Before You Ask

Common questions from buyers new to Dubai.

Can a foreigner own a villa in Dubai outright?+
Yes, in designated freehold areas, which cover every villa in this collection. You hold full title, and you can sell, lease, or pass it on exactly as you would anywhere else.
Does buying a villa qualify me for the Golden Visa?+
Properties above AED 2 million qualify the owner for a ten year renewable visa. We file the application alongside the purchase, so it moves in parallel rather than after.
Is a payment plan available, or is it cash only?+
Most villas in the collection are resale, so payment is typically in full at transfer. Where a villa is still under construction, we lay out the developer's plan clearly before you commit to anything.
How long from offer to holding the keys?+
For a resale villa, transfer through the Dubai Land Department usually takes two to four weeks once terms are agreed. New builds follow the developer's own handover date, which we confirm before you sign anything.
